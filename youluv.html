YouLuv

Kinder self-talk, in your own voice.

A private affirmations app for iOS. Record short affirmations by speaking into your phone, or generate them on-device from a few keywords and hear them spoken back in your Personal Voice. No accounts, no servers, no analytics.

01 — Why

Two reasons, one app

Personal: I wanted a simple, private way to fight my own negative self-talk. Something that lived on my phone, that I owned, and that would let me hear kinder things said back to me — sometimes in my own voice.

Technical: I wanted to see whether on-device intelligence could actually help someone in a small, honest way. No cloud round-trips, no data collection, no subscription — just Apple's on-device language model and Personal Voice doing something quietly useful.

02 — What it does

A short list, on purpose

The app is small by design. Fewer surfaces, fewer permissions, fewer reasons not to open it.

Record short, first-person affirmations by speaking into your phone.

Generate affirmations from a few keywords using Apple's on-device language model, and hear them read aloud in your Personal Voice.

Play back any affirmation from a chronological list, filter by category, and get reminders at times you choose so the list actually gets used.

Everything runs on-device. Devices without Apple Intelligence fall back gracefully — you can still record and play back manually.

03 — Under the hood

SwiftUI, SwiftData, on-device only

Built in Swift and SwiftUI, targeting iOS 17.5+ with a paused macOS target. Persistence is SwiftData. Generation uses Apple's Foundation Models framework where available, with a graceful fallback path when it isn't. Playback in your own voice uses Personal Voice through AVSpeechSynthesizer.

Permission prompts (microphone, notifications) only fire when you take the action that needs them — never on launch. The app owns its data and its behavior; nothing leaves the device.

01 — About

First The Human Being

I'm a full blown self proclaimed nerd. I like comics, video games and flying drones. My knowledge of Spider-Man is impressive and I will never not talk about Batman. I'm not afraid to make sure to lift a heavy weight, a pull up bar or go for a walk because I'm not getting any younger. Ok with that out the way lets move on to technology.

Systems thinking, applied broadly

I've spent over a decade moving from support and consulting roles into senior engineering, where I now help set standards as a Development and Engineering Chair. My core strength is event-driven architecture — designing systems around Kafka, RESTful APIs, and .NET/C# that stay maintainable as they scale.

Lately that's extended into AI-integrated systems: retrieval-augmented search with pgvector, local inference with Ollama, and thinking seriously about where automation is resilient versus where it's brittle. I care less about being first to a new framework and more about understanding why a system is built the way it is — which is usually the difference between code that works today and architecture that survives five years of change.

Outside the backend, I build for iOS including three published App Store apps, with SwiftUI and SwiftData work spanning offline first persistence, testing, and app security. I hold a Google Cybersecurity certificate and I'm actively building toward AppSec and staff-level engineering.
